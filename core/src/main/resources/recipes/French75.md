# French 75

## Ingredients

- [OPTIONAL] Elderflower cordial (10 ml)
- Gin (50 ml)
- Ice cubes
- Lime
- Champagne/Prosecco/Cava

## Method

1. Add the ice cubes to a small tumbler.
1. Pour the gin and elderflower cordial to the glass.
1. Chop the lime into segments. Piece a segment with a fork and squeeze the lime juice into the glass. Once juiced, drop the lime segment into the glass.
1. Top up the glass with sparkling wine.
