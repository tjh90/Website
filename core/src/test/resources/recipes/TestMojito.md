# Mojito

## Ingredients

- [OPTIONAL] Fresh mint leaves (10 leaves)
- White rum (60 ml)
- Lime
- Sugar/Simple syrup (2 teaspoons)
- Soda water

## Method

1. Muddle the mint leaves and lime with the sugar or syrup.
1. Pour in the white rum and fill the glass with ice.
1. Top up with soda water and stir.
