# Spaghetti Carbonara

## Ingredients

- Spaghetti (200 g)
- [OPTIONAL] Guanciale (80 g)
- Eggs (2)
- Pecorino/Parmigiano (handful)

## Method

1. Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente.
1. Fry the guanciale until crisp.
1. Whisk the eggs with the grated cheese, then toss with the hot spaghetti and guanciale.
1. Season with plenty of black pepper.
