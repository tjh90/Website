# Chocolate Monstrosity

## Ingredients

- Maltesers (200 g)

- Dark chocolate (200 mg)
- Milk chocolate (200 mg)
- White chocolate (200 mg)

- Double cream (750 ml)

## Method

1. Line a tin with greaseproof paper. Preheat the oven to 180 °C.
1. Crush the maltesers and cover the base of the tin. It should be up to half a cm deep.
1. Bake for 20-30 mins until the base has melted and leave to cool.
1. For each type of chocolate, break into smaller blocks and melt using one of the following methods:
    - Using a microwave: use a low power setting and heat the chocolate in short bursts (_e.g._ 30 s) checking and stirring between cycles.
    - Using a bain-marie: place the chocolate in a heatproof bowl. Position the bowl over a pot of simmering water, ensuring the bowl is not in contact with the water. Leave to melt without stirring.
1. Once the chocolate has melted, set it aside to cool until at the temperature of a pleasant shower.
1. Whip the cream until nearly stiff. It should be holding shape, but not firm.
1. Mix a third of the cream into each type of chocolate, carefully folding it in without beating it.
1. Add a layer of dark, white, then milk chocolate to the tin.
1. Chill for 1-2, maybe 4 hours.
1. Remove from the tin with care and decorate with any leftover maltesers that have escaped being eaten by the baker.
