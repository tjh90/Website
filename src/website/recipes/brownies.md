# Brownies

## Ingredients

- Butter (120 g)
- Dark chocolate (180 g)
- Milk chocolate (60 g)

- Eggs (3, at room temperature)
- Light brown soft sugar (210 g)

- Plain flour (75 g)
- Cocoa powder (30 g)

- White chocolate/Milk chocolate (200 g)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 160 °C and line a 20 cm square tin with baking paper (cut large enough to allow an overhang to facilitate removal).
1. Melt the chocolate and butter together in a bain-marie (in a heatproof bowl over a pan of simmering water). Set aside to cool until at the temperature of a pleasant shower.
1. Using an electric mixer, whisk the eggs and sugar on medium-high speed for 5-7 mins until pale, fluffy, and about tripled in volume. The mixture should briefly pile on top of itself as it falls off the whisk.
1. Fold the melted chocolate in until only just incorporated.
1. Sift the flour an cocoa powder into the chocolate mixture and gently fold in.
1. Stir in the white chocolate chunks, raspberries, or other sugary detritus and gently fold in.
1. Put the mixture in the tin and bake for 24-48 mins (depending on how set they should be) or until a toothpick inserted into the brownie comes out covered in half-baked batter (gooey) or many moist crumbs (fudgy).
1. Allow to cool to room temperature before attempting removal from the tin.
