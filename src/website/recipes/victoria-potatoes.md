# Victoria Potatoes

## Ingredients

- Potatoes (1 per person)

- Cooking oil

- [OPTIONAL] Garlic (crushed)
- [OPTIONAL] Onions (finely chopped)
- [OPTIONAL] Rosemary

## Method

1. Peel the potatoes.
1. Chop into 3-4 cm pieces.
1. Cover all pieces liberally in oil.
1. Cook at 180 °C fan for 45-50 mins. If you can stand it, baste part way through.
