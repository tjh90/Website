# Tim Pasta

## Ingredients

- [OPTIONAL] Bacon (2-3 rashers)
- [OPTIONAL] Sausages (2-3)

- Bird's eye chilis (2 per person)
- [OPTIONAL] Broccoli
- Carrots (2-3)
- Onions (1)
- [OPTIONAL] Peppers (1)

- Cooking oil
- Italian style herb blend
- [OPTIONAL] Raisins

- [OPTIONAL] Cashew nuts
- Mustard (1 tbsp)
- Peas
- Worcestershire sauce

- Cider vinegar

- Pasta
- Cheese (a generous handful)

## Method

1. If using bacon or sausage, cook in the oven at 180 °C for 20 mins.
1. Dice the broccoli, carrots, chilis, onion, and peppers.
1. Throw all the diced stuff into a pan with some cooking oil. Add the italian style herbs (feel free to supplement with other herbs or spices selected at random) and raisins if using them. Fry on a medium-high heat for 10-15 mins.
    - When the bacon and sausage are cooked, chop them and add them to the pan.
1. After 5-10 mins, add the peas and cashew nuts. Add the mustard and Worcestershire sauce.
1. A few mins before the end, add the cider vinegar to the pan.
1. Reduce the frying pan to a low heat and cook the pasta. Grate the cheese.
1. Once the pasta is cooked, add it to the frying pan and stir the cheese in. Cook for another few mins before staining and serving.
