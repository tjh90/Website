# Chicken Stew

## Ingredients

- Carrots
- Celery (1-2 sticks)
- Onions (1-2)

- Chicken
- White wine/Sherry

- Parsnips
- Sweet potatoes
- Tarragon
- Thyme

- Gravy
- Mustard

- Chicken stock

- Bay leaf
- [OPTIONAL] Parmesan rind
## Method

1. Finely chop an onion, 1-2 carrots, and 1-2 sticks of celery. Fry on a medium heat until softened and translucent. Remove from the pan and set aside.
1. Prepare the chicken:
    - If using chicken leftover from a roast, extract whatever meat you can from the carcass. Consider making stock to use later.
    - If using raw chicken (thighs are preferred), flour and season. Brown in the cooking pot, then set aside. You may wish to deglaze the pan with sherry (alternatively, white wine or brandy can be used).
1. Peel and chop some additional root vegetables (parsnips, sweet potatoes, carrots) and add into the pot along with the softened vegetable mix. Add a healthy dash of tarragon and thyme.
1. Add white wine (or sherry). Especially if no deglazing has been done.
1. Turn up the heat and bubble for 2-3 minutes.
1. Reduce the heat and add 1-2 tsp of mustard, and 1-2 tsp of chutney, or leftover gravy.
1. Add stock to cover the contents of the pot.
1. Add the bay leaf and parmesan rind.
1. Simmer on a low heat for an hour. If using raw chicken, make sure the chicken is cooked through and falling off the bone.
1. Add additional green veg (_e.g._ cabbage) about 10 mins before the end.
