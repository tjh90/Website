# Bangers and Mash

## Ingredients

- Sausages (2-3 per person)

- Potatoes (1 per person)

- Onions (1-2)

- Milk
- Mustard (1 tbsp)
- Cheese (100g, grated)

## Method

1. Put the sausages in the oven and cook at 180 °C for 20 mins.
1. Peel and chop the potatoes. Boil for 20 mins.
1. Dice the onions. Fry in a pan on a low heat.
1. Once the sausages are done, chop them and add them to the pan along with any chutneys and herbs to hand.
1. Drain the water from the potato pot. Mash the potatoes, adding a generous dash of milk, the mustard, and three quarters of the cheese. Stir in some of the onions if that takes your fancy.
1. Garnish the mash with the remaining cheese once served.
