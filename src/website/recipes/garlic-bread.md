# Garlic Bread

## Ingredients

- Butter (50 g)
- Garlic (4-6 cloves)
- Cheese (30-50 g)

- Bread (1 baguette)

## Method

1. Crush the garlic. Mix the butter, garlic, and parmesan in a bowl.
1. Slice into the baguette (without bisecting it) to create tearable slices.
1. Add the butter mixture in between the slices. Smear a small amount over the top of the baguette.
1. Wrap the baguette in foil with a join at the top. Set the oven to 180 °C.
    - If using a fresh loaf, bake for 10 mins.
    - If using a parbaked loaf, bake for 20 mins.
1. Peel back the foil and bake for another 10 mins until crust is golden brown in colour.
